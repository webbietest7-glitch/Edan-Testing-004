A garden can be more than a place to admire nature. It can be a place to learn from it.

We see the garden as a space for observation, experimentation, and discovery — a place to explore how living systems work and how people can live more thoughtfully alongside them.

From the smallest leaf to the wider ecosystem, there is always something to notice, question, and understand.
